# About

I'm Olivia Coleman (they/she), a librarian and digital humanist scholar with a background in applied mathematics, information science, and interdisciplinary humanities scholarship.

I currently supervise Library Curriculum Support Services at Princeton University Library. My previous roles include academic library access services at Purchase College and Columbia University Libraries.

I earned an MS in Information Science at the University at Albany and am currently pursuing graduate study in Global Digital Humanities at the University of St Andrews. My research focuses on digital scholarship, metadata, knowledge organization, and how institutions communicate and represent information.

I also maintain independent creative and scholarly projects, including [The Lesbian Taxonomy Project](https://tltp.page/).
